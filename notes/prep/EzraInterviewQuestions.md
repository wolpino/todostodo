# Ezra Interview — Questions to Ask the Panel

**Pick 3–4 total.** Mix system curiosity with role clarity. Avoid perks/benefits in this round.

---

## For Kristina Matiukhina (Team Lead)

Kristina's background: QA → data engineering → fullstack; HIPAA/PHI at Klick; data quality focus; ~3 years at Ezra (Vue, .NET, Azure).

1. **Pod ownership:** "What does your pod own day-to-day — Hub, scheduling, or reporter — and where would this role land initially?"

2. **Quality bar:** "Given your background in QA and data quality, what does 'done' look like for a feature before it reaches members?"

3. **Post-acquisition:** "You've been on the team through Ezra's growth — what's changed most in how your pod ships since the Function acquisition?"

4. **First 90 days:** "What would success look like for someone joining your pod in the first 90 days?"

5. **Cross-functional:** "How does engineering collaborate with clinical and medical provider partners when requirements are ambiguous?"

---

## For Michael Hambor (Staff Engineer)

Michael's background: Staff at Ezra; ex-Klick complex data platform architect; trusted for full-stack prod incidents; "make sure it gets done right."

1. **Architecture bets:** "Where do you see the biggest architectural bets for the imaging platform in the next year?"

2. **Incidents:** "What does a well-run production incident look like on your team — from detection to postmortem?"

3. **Staff role:** "How do staff engineers stay hands-on without becoming the only person who can unblock hard problems?"

4. **Design constraints:** "When you architect a new service here, what constraints do you always design for upfront — HIPAA, partner SLAs, something else?"

5. **Lessons learned:** "What's an example of a production incident that taught the team something durable?"

## General (either interviewer)

### Reporter pipeline
"How does the Reporter pod handle the pipeline from radiologist report → consumer-friendly output? Where does human review sit in that flow?"

### On-call
"What's your on-call model for the imaging platform, and what does a typical incident look like?"

### Compliance
"How do you balance shipping fast with HIPAA/compliance constraints — review gates, audit logging patterns?"

### Function integration
"How does the planned Function integration pod change the architecture you're building toward?"

### 90-day success
"What would success look like for this role in the first 90 days on the imaging team?"

## Questions to avoid

- Compensation, PTO, remote policy (recruiter stage)
- "What's the tech stack?" (you know it — shows you didn't research)
- Anything answerable from the website alone
