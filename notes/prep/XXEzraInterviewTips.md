# These are comments from an engineer that already went through this interview:

- It was largely behavioral and technical ex: “what would you do if you had this production issue?” to see your thought process.

- 30 minutes towards the end to walk through your thought process when working on the take home. Make sure you actually know what you did and tradeoffs.

- they asked some "what if" questions about my choices, and why. I went through each problem, how I diagnosed it, and the fix.

- The engineer that did not pass this interview thinks they didn't have enough support background. They asked a lot of questions about their experience with production incidents.
